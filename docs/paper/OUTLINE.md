## Title candidates

1. **Efficiency and Wildness Are Jointly Produced: A Three-Way Refutation in
   Minimal Market Models** (primary)
2. The Price of Information-Free Prices: Why Rational Flow Cannot Separate
   Efficiency from Fat Tails
3. The 5/10 Ceiling: Stylized Facts a Minimal Market Cannot Buy

## The one-sentence claim (scoped)

In flow-based minimal markets with boundedly-rational agents — trader,
iterated-anticipation, and price-setting classes — sign-efficiency and the
wild stylized facts (fat tails, leverage asymmetry, volatility clustering)
are jointly produced by the same state-riding flows and cannot be purchased
separately; removing the sign leak removes the wildness, at every rationality
depth tested.

**Explicitly NOT claimed**: anything about real markets' generating process;
anything beyond this model class (no learning agents, no heterogeneous
information, no strategic equilibrium/rational-expectations solution);
optimality of the agent designs (each is gate-verified to *function*, not to
be optimal).

   attempts to reproduce them (LeBaron; Lux–Marchesi); the standing gap —
   no battery-complete scoring, no pre-registered refutation chain. Our
   contribution: (i) a 10-event battery calibrated so real SPY scores 10/10
   and GBM exactly 3/10; (ii) a three-experiment pre-registered arc whose
   *refutations* compose into a structural claim; (iii) the
   convict-and-exonerate gate methodology that makes negative results
   evidential.
2. **The battery and the base market** (~3 pp). The 10 events (from DESIGN8);
   calibration; the FCVM configuration and its 5/10 ceiling; the five
   missing events as diagnosis (long memory, arrow-in-coupling,
   information-free signs). Figure: battery scorecard, SPY vs GBM vs FCVM.
3. **Experiment I — expectation (DECA2)** (~2 pp). Anticipatory agent
   front-running forecastable vol-targeting flow; causal by construction
   (X30b); result: score unchanged, the sign leak reappears one derivative
   earlier. Figure: E9 bits before/after.
4. **Experiment II — the fixed point (DECA3)** (~2.5 pp). Iterating
   anticipation K=5; the algebraic identity (the K-stack is one anticipator
   of strength 1−(1−k_A)^K); the inversion: forecastable-flow fraction falls
   exactly as (1−k_A)^K while leaked bits RISE (0.0184→0.0257). Open-loop
   contraction dies in closed-loop equilibrium. Figure: the two opposing
   curves vs K — the paper's centerpiece plot.
5. **Experiment III — price-setting (DECA4)** (~2.5 pp). The maker that
   provably absorbs the leak (toy corr +0.658→−0.058, flow unchanged —
   X34c); result: E9 persists, and at full skew the wild facts die instead
   (kurt 8.8→3.0, leverage −0.125→−0.008). The joint-production reading.
   Figure: wild-fact medians vs skew λ, with E9 bits overlaid.
6. **The structural claim and its limits** (~1.5 pp). The closure clause as
   methodology (pre-registered end of the line); what "structural" means
   here and what would falsify it (an agent class outside the three, e.g.
   learning or information-heterogeneous agents — named as open, not
   hedged).
7. **Empirical counterpart — the microstructure triangle** (~2 pp). The
   leverage effect across equity cohort (−0.04) / FX (+0.005 ≈ 0) / crypto

   licenses the reading; the equity-edge certified (z=3.9), the FX–crypto
   edge honestly inside noise (z=1.44). Positioning: consistent with the
   thesis (the wildness channel follows the flow structure), not proof of it.
8. **Methods** (~2 pp). Convict-and-exonerate gates (size AND power on

## Abstract (draft v1)

> Agent-based models reproduce financial stylized facts piecemeal; whether a
> single minimal mechanism can buy them all is open. We score minimal
> flow-based markets against a ten-event battery calibrated so that real
> equity data passes all ten events and geometric Brownian motion exactly
> three. A market of fundamentalists, chartists, volatility-targeters and
> market-makers reaches five of ten, and its failures are informative: the
> missing events concern long memory, time-irreversibility, and
> information-free price signs. We then test, in three pre-registered
> experiments with kill criteria, whether rationality can close the gap:
> (i) an anticipatory agent that front-runs the forecastable flow; (ii) the
> fixed point of mutual anticipation, iterated to depth five; (iii) a
> price-setting maker that provably absorbs the forecastable component into
> the price level. All three fail, and they fail informatively: deeper
> anticipation *grows* the sign leak while contracting the flow it targets,
> and exact absorption kills the fat tails, leverage asymmetry and
> volatility clustering along with the leak. We conclude that in this model
> class, sign-efficiency and the wild stylized facts are jointly produced by
> the same state-riding flows and are not separately purchasable. An
> empirical counterpart — the leverage effect's sign ordered by market
> microstructure across equities (−0.04), FX (≈0), and crypto (+0.03) — is
> consistent with the mechanism. All results are pre-registered,
> gate-verified against synthetic ground truth, and regenerable from the
> public repository.
